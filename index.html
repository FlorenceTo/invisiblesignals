Invisible Signals is an experimental radio series, structured as a live progression through live electronics, field recordings, and archival fragments. Taking its title from waves that remain imperceptible even as they are heard and felt, the series attends to signal as a materially distributed condition of vibration, shaped through bodies, environments, and infrastructural systems. Each episode moves between sonic essay, radio play, and live composition, examining rupture, resistance, and repair across borders, technological infrastructures, and contested atmospheres. This is the first series hosted by Florence To.
